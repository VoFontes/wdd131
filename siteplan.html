Website Planning Document

Site Name

Discover Sonora

I chose the name Discover Sonora because the purpose of the website is to help visitors discover the culture, food, landscapes, history, and beautiful places of Sonora, Mexico. The name is simple, easy to remember, and directly communicates what the website is about.

Site Purpose

Discover Sonora will provide information about Sonora, Mexico, including interesting places to visit, traditional foods, cultural traditions, landscapes, and other important characteristics of the state.

The website will help visitors learn more about Sonora and discover places they may want to visit. It will also provide useful information for people who are interested in learning about the culture and natural beauty of the region.

Scenarios

  • What are some of the most beautiful places to visit in Sonora, Mexico?
  • What traditional foods should I try when visiting Sonora?
  • What cultural traditions and activities can I experience in Sonora?

Color Schema

Deep Teal

#1F4E5F

Used for the header, navigation, major headings, and other important elements.

Desert Gold

#F4B942

Used for accents, buttons, borders, highlights, and decorative elements.

Light Background

#F5F5F5

Used for the main page background and areas that need a light, clean appearance.

Dark Text

#333333

Used for paragraphs and general text to provide good readability.

Typography

Roboto

Roboto will be used for the main headings, section headings, navigation elements, and important titles.

Open Sans

Open Sans will be used for body paragraphs, descriptions, lists, and general website content.
