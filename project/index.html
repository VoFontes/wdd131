A Land of Contrasts and Traditions

Located in northwestern Mexico, Sonora offers a remarkable combination of desert landscapes, mountains, coastal communities, and vibrant cities. Its geography and history have helped shape a distinctive regional identity.

From the shores of the Sea of Cortez to the vast Sonoran Desert, every region offers something different to discover. Sonora is also known for its regional cuisine, hospitality, and cultural traditions.
