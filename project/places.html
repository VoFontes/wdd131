Discover Sonora's Landscapes

From the Sea of Cortez to the Sonoran Desert, explore the natural beauty and destinations of northwestern Mexico.

Places Worth Discovering

Sonora offers a variety of landscapes and experiences. Visitors can enjoy coastal scenery, explore desert environments, and discover communities with distinctive histories and traditions.

San Carlos

San Carlos, near Guaymas, is known for its coastal scenery, clear waters, and views of the distinctive Tetakawi mountain. Visitors enjoy boating, fishing, kayaking, and exploring the surrounding natural environment.

Puerto Peñasco

Located on the northern coast of the Sea of Cortez, Puerto Peñasco is a popular coastal destination. Its beaches, sunsets, seafood, and opportunities for outdoor recreation make it an attractive place to visit.

The Sonoran Desert

The Sonoran Desert is recognized for its remarkable biodiversity and iconic saguaro cacti. Its plants and animals have adapted to challenging conditions, creating a unique natural environment.

Hermosillo

Hermosillo is the capital and one of the largest cities in Sonora. It offers museums, parks, regional restaurants, and opportunities to learn about the state's history and modern culture.
